        Grid

        Squares follow the MGRS grid in the package's UTM zone. Pick a square to split it into smaller squares, then tap or drag across squares to black them out as not of interest.

        Residential blackout

        Draw and Box black out new ground. Cut out opens a hole. Erase removes a whole shape. Reshape moves one shape's corners. The opacity also applies to blacked-out grid squares.
